## What I work on

**Now —** Systems architecture at the intersection of hardware and software. At c-sense I define interface contracts, async command routing patterns, and hardware abstraction strategies over vendor-specific APIs. Outside of work, I run my own Kubernetes infrastructure (lyeh-infra) and explore DevOps/GitOps/MLOps patterns end-to-end.

**In the AI era —** I believe architects need solid foundational skills to remain effective. Even when AI can produce code faster, you need the depth to judge whether it's correct — especially at the systems level. In DevOps and automation, AI is actually a force multiplier: it handles the repetitive details, freeing me to focus on whether the architecture is sound.

**Always sharpening —** I develop lib/api/service projects through real, complex systems to build genuine architecture intuition. Right now I'm working through Linux kernel internals, implementing KFS from scratch — the most complex real-world architecture I know, and the most interesting challenge.
